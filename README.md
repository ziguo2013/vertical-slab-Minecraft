Do you feel that the types of vertical slabs in the original game are too limited to meet your specific building needs? This mod is just what you need!

The Vertical Slabs mod adds a series of vertical slab variants not included in the original game. Therefore, we have created the corresponding vertical slabs. All new blocks strictly adhere to the original game's style and crafting logic, and they support all resource packs. You can craft them simply by placing the corresponding materials vertically in the crafting table. This will directly fill the gap in the original game, providing you with more choices when building window frames, partitions, and decorative details.

“竖半砖”模组添加了一系列原版游戏中尚未包含的竖半砖变体。所以我们制作了对应的竖半砖。所有新增方块都严格遵循原版的风格和合成逻辑，并且支持所有材质包，您只需使用对应材料的竖着在工作台摆放即可合成。这将直接弥补原版的空白，为您在建造窗框、隔断、装饰线条时提供更丰富的选择。
